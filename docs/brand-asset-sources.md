# Brand assets and product previews

September 13, 2026: Vanteloq AI now uses a resolution-independent V-and-orbit component based on the owner's supplied `Futuristic Vanteloq AI Neon Banner.png`. The faceted blue V, tilted ring, illuminated edge and orbital light are code-native vectors, with restrained purple highlights to match the existing theme. The shared component serves navigation, chat, thinking, retail prompts and product previews. The homepage's matching presentation runs as decorative motion with plain OpenAI attribution and no playback or thinking-preview controls; it makes no AI request. The connected-workspace story cycles through clearly labelled fictional sales, inventory, cash and comparison examples, with owner review preserved. Compact marks remain quiet until hover or activity, and reduced-motion preferences stop all animation. Provider attribution remains plain text and does not imply an endorsement or automated action capabilities.

Verified on September 7, 2026.

- Google: unmodified current gradient G from Google's identity documentation, https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png. Guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines. The mark retains its aspect ratio and white backing.
- Meta: unmodified Meta vector from the Simple Icons library, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/meta. Compared with Meta's published corporate mark at https://about.fb.com/news/2021/10/facebook-company-is-now-meta/. This replaces the approximate path previously drawn in the application. No recolouring or stretching is applied.
- Both assets are served locally. Loading a logo does not send a visitor request to either provider. Provider names identify integrations, not a partnership or endorsement.
- The platform preview reuses `IntradaySalesChart` and `CashPositionRing`, the components used in the authenticated product. All example records are fictional, fixed and labelled. It never reads sessions or calls business APIs. Missing costs and negative cash balances remain visible as distinct states.
- Brand permissions and provider approvals remain separate from technical integration readiness. The previews do not imply that every connector is operational.
