# Account and workspace deletion review

Reviewed October 4, 2026. This is source and isolated-test evidence, not a production deletion, provider revocation exercise, or legal compliance certification.

## Implemented scope and corrections

- Settings, Account and login, and `/account/deletion` expose the existing permanent-deletion workflow. An owner confirms deletion of the selected workspace; a nonowner confirms their personal account/membership. Billing cancellation is a separate Settings control and does not itself delete the workspace.
- Requests require authenticated tenant access, recent MFA, an exact confirmation phrase, irreversibility acknowledgement, and a separate immediate-billing-cancellation acknowledgement for workspace deletion. No paid subscription is required. The current UI displays the reviewed workspace name and sends its `expectedOrganizationId`; a changed workspace is rejected before any deletion job is created. The additional request field is backwards compatible for existing API callers.
- Corrected stale authority: ownership, active identity and membership scope are checked before identity preparation, immediately before billing termination, before file cleanup, and within the atomic local database batch. A previously issued private capability does not authorize deletion after ownership loss or a move to another workspace. The local stage CHECK aborts the complete batch when its scope or processing lease no longer matches.
- Corrected personal cleanup: notification contents, onboarding drafts, saved forecast view preferences and workspace session records are explicitly deleted. The existing user tombstone remains to preserve business-record foreign keys; it no longer has the user's name, email or authentication subject. Other members' personal records and the employer's business records are preserved.
- Corrected temporary document-copy handling: active scan/extraction claims, saved provider-copy references, pending document deletions and unresolved upload intents prevent workspace confirmation. The user can finish the existing Documents processing/cleanup workflow while the request remains unconfirmed. The database confirmation and document processing claim are mutually guarded, so late processing cannot start after deletion is confirmed. Checks repeat before file cleanup and in the local deletion batch. Cleanup references are never silently discarded by workspace cascade.

## Cleanup inventory and boundaries

`server/account-deletion.ts` removes tenant R2 objects under the workspace prefix, then deletes legacy tenant vehicle/task/audit rows and the workspace. Foreign-key cascades remove the current tenant-owned accounting, imported commerce, document, integration credential, membership, dealership, food-service and operational records. Document upload intents deliberately do not cascade; readiness requires them to be resolved first. The requesting user's unrelated identities and other members' independent Supabase identities are preserved. Isolated fixtures cover tenant separation; this review did not populate every table with every possible cross-reference.

Workspace deletion requires ordinary integrations to be disconnected first, verifies cancellation of the stored Vanteloq Stripe subscription/customer before local data removal, and reconciles a checkout completed after the initial billing snapshot. The identity bridge verifies its target against the immutable server plan and confirms removal through readback. An interrupted identity step stays pending and can resume without the deleted workspace login. No new provider credentials or production deletion requests were used.

Cloud-file disconnection and applicable account/workspace deletion remove local credentials and linked-source records. This does not universally revoke provider-side app grants. The existing privacy policy instructs users to remove the app in the provider account where necessary. Independent providers' legally retained records are outside this application's deletion result. This increment preserves that limitation and displays it before confirmation.

The encrypted retry job lasts 30 days. Completed jobs clear the target user/workspace identifiers and encrypted plan. Pseudonymous receipts have a two-year expiry, and expired receipts/completed jobs are removed during deletion-request housekeeping. This is not a continuously scheduled purge. Minimal email suppression and unlinked consent evidence follow their separate purpose-limited policy. Expired unresolved jobs require verified Privacy Officer assistance.

## Legal and policy review

Federal PIPEDA, Schedule 1, clauses 4.5, 4.5.2 and 4.5.3 require retention tied to identified purposes, retention/disposal procedures, and disposal or anonymization when the purpose ends; they also recognize decision-access and other legislative retention requirements. This supports truthful scoped deletion and documented exceptions, not a universal promise to erase every record immediately. [Current federal Act](https://lois-laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/p-8.6/page-7.html), [Office of the Privacy Commissioner guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_use/).

Alberta PIPA is provincial private-sector legislation. The Alberta government's retention guidance says organizations should keep personal information only while reasonable for business or legal purposes, then securely destroy it or make it anonymous. Applicability depends on the organization and activity; this review does not substitute one statute for the other. [Alberta government guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/organization-responsibilities-for-protecting-personal-information).

The repository's `docs/DATA_RETENTION.md`, `docs/compliance/DATA_RETENTION_AND_DISPOSAL_POLICY.md` and public Privacy Policy distinguish active records, backups, provider records, legal holds and limited deletion evidence. The operating policy requires managed backup lifecycle controls and reapplication of deletion tombstones after restoration. This review has no evidence of a production backup expiration or restore-and-reapply exercise. The UI therefore states that managed backups follow retention policy and does not claim immediate backup erasure. Legal-hold, dispute, quarterly-review and provider-grant checks remain operational responsibilities where the application has no automated enforcement or live evidence. A generic deletion receipt does not establish that such a review happened.
