Owners can inspect performance, follow a total to permitted records, review work requiring attention and maintain a dashboard suited to their business.

This record supports a controlled release of implemented workflows, not completion of every requested accounting, restaurant or dealership system. ## Mathematical and source rules

| Measure | Definition and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Net revenue | Completed sales less eligible refunds; excludes tax; selected authority and date/location/currency. |
| Gross profit | Eligible net revenue less matching recorded COGS. Unknown cost stays unknown. |
| Gross margin | Gross profit ÷ eligible net revenue ×100; explicit zero/missing denominator. Change is percentage points. |
| Average order value | Net revenue ÷ matching completed transaction count; same cohort. |
| Net units | Sold units less matching returned units; not stock or money. |
| Contribution | Revenue less explicitly included variable costs; not net profit. |
| Cash | Current eligible bank snapshot or ledger cash, separately labelled with as-of time; signed zero/overdraft. |
| AR/AP aging | Eligible unsettled balances by stated due-date/as-of policy; document match is not settlement. |
| Recipe cost | Compatible quantities × recorded cost, with yield/portions; unknown modifier blocks completeness. |
| Production loss | Unusable prepared portions ÷ prepared portions, weighted by cohort. |
| Holding estimate | Entered daily minor units × whole calendar days; distinct from posted cost. |
| Forecast | Supported observations and explicit scenario inputs/horizon/uncertainty; not accounting fact. |

Money uses exact minor-unit arithmetic within safe ranges; unsafe totals are withheld. CAD/USD cannot be silently combined. No FX rate is inferred. Unknown differs from zero; incomplete imports do not prove closure.

Comparisons require matching timezones, periods, locations, sources, cohorts and currencies. Stable IDs, idempotency, atomic batches, versions/generations and uniqueness protect repeat/partial processing. They cannot guarantee the accuracy of customer-entered information.
