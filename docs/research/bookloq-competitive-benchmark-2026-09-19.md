# BookLoQ competitive capability benchmark

Accessed September 19, 2026.

This benchmark uses official product pages and first-party help documentation. It separates documented capabilities from published interface observations, measured performance and BookLoQ recommendations. It is not a hands-on speed, accuracy or paid-account usability test.

## Current BookLoQ baseline

BookLoQ's public page documents posted journals flowing into the trial balance, profit and loss statement and balance sheet; imported transaction review and matching; a month-end checklist; and a 13-week cash forecast that separates confirmed commitments from expected receipts. Vanteloq AI can explain permitted summaries, but it cannot post journals or approve payments. QuickBooks connectivity remains sandbox-only.

Source: [BookLoQ Accounting and Cash Flow for Small Businesses](https://vanteloq.com/features/financial-review).

## Product conclusions

1. Preserve the existing BookLoQ ledger, transaction review and matching, month-end and 13-week cash capabilities. Deepen them instead of describing them as missing.
2. Make sale-to-bank lineage a core commerce workflow: sale, refund, tax, fee, reserve, payout, deposit, journal and financial statement.
3. Use an evidence-led review queue instead of a chatbot as the primary accounting interaction.
4. Keep original documents beside extracted or proposed accounting values.
5. Distinguish capture, suggestion, review, approval, posting, payment and period locking.
6. Show source freshness, mapping, approval and missing-data status before presenting a report as ready.
7. Keep forecasting explainable through assumptions, conservative and planning cases, thresholds and decision sensitivity.
8. Do not claim that BookLoQ is faster, more accurate or universally better until representative tasks are measured with a disclosed method.
