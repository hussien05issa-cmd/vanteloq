# Vanteloq Interface Direction

User-approved direction, September 14, 2026. Applies to public pages and the authenticated workspace, including BookLoQ, AI, settings, integrations and forms.

- Keep Vanteloq's navy and blue identity. Use smooth, restrained gradients, subtle glossy highlights and frosted navigation. Keep records, charts and inputs opaque and off-white.
- Use Geist and the existing vector icon family. Left-align headings, copy and labels. Right-align comparable financial values. Use tabular numerals and numbers for counts and steps.
- Group related controls with consistent spacing. Give each section 1 job. Keep detailed exploration on /demo; the homepage should introduce it rather than duplicate it.
- Use clear actions, active voice and title case for short interface labels. Keep explanatory prose in sentence case. Explain technical concepts in everyday language.
- Use navigation targets of at least 44px, 48px form controls, 12px control corners and 20px panels. Distinguish selected navigation with an edge or shape as well as colour.
- Keep entry forms in 1 column. Mark required fields with an asterisk and optional fields with (Optional). Preserve native validation, place errors beside fields and focus the first invalid field.
- Format structured inputs such as verification codes, Canadian postal codes and ISO currency codes. Do not impose Canadian telephone formatting on international users or change accounting precision.
- Keep onboarding in steps with accessible progress. Reveal secondary settings when needed and preserve entries when moving back.
- Use skeleton loading for predictable layouts. Keep loading, empty, unavailable and error states distinct. Missing data is not zero.
- Preserve AI consent, memory, deletion, financial calculations, permissions, source lineage, provider boundaries and billing behaviour.
- Keep mobile cookie settings in normal page flow after the content, clear of chat controls and other actions.
- Respect reduced motion, reduced transparency and forced colours. Decorative effects must not be needed to interpret a result.

## Research

- [Apple: Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials). Restrained effects for navigation and controls; legibility takes priority.
- [W3C: Accessible Forms](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/). Associated labels, useful instructions and understandable errors.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Contrast, focus, navigation, target size and status messages. This work is not a claim of complete WCAG certification.
- [Nielsen Norman Group: Visibility of System Status](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visibility-system-status/). Tell users what is happening and what they can do next.
- [Nielsen Norman Group: Closeness of Actions and Objects](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/closeness-of-actions-and-objects-gui/). Place actions beside the data or item they affect.

Use the attached broad brief as a reference. Reuse existing features, remove repetition and support claims with implemented behaviour.
