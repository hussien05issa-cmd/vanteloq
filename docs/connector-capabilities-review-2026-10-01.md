## Existing architecture to extend

Source anchors: `app/integration-catalog.ts`, `domain/integration-availability.ts`, `domain/integration-capabilities.ts`, `domain/provider-feature-coverage.ts`, `domain/provider-report-contracts.ts`, `domain/provider-privacy.ts`, `domain/integration-source.ts`, `server/integrations/`, `server/integrations/sync/`, `server/integrations/sync-policy.ts`, `server/linked-files.ts` and the corresponding `app/api/v1/` routes.

The capability registry describes inbound, outbound and confirmed actions. It correctly distinguishes Stripe/Moneris/Deel review data, QuickBooks company identity, Slack links, and planned commerce reports. Preserve those boundaries. `CanonicalCommerceCoverage` currently uses seven entity-presence booleans; feature coverage uses `ready | needs_data`. Report contracts additionally distinguish `available | planned`, and source selection has its own readiness/authority rules. Those are useful foundations, but entity presence alone cannot prove complete costs, populated identities, expiry dates, a comparison period, or current authorization.

Extend each metric contract with required fields and semantics; allowed source roles; grant/resource coverage; requested and imported interval; pagination completion; correction version; currency and location/time-zone basis; freshness; quality exclusions; and a reasoned state of `supported`, `missing`, `partial`, `stale`, `not_authorised`, or `not_applicable`. Keep implementation status, production approval and customer data readiness separate. A stale provider grant cannot become “zero sales.” An empty, fully imported period can be zero only when completeness and the metric definition establish it.

Examples: gross profit needs period net sales and attributable cost; expiry exposure needs lot/expiry/quantity evidence; repeat purchase needs stable customer links and adequate history; turnover needs period COGS and defensible average inventory; aging needs due dates, credits and allocations; a cash forecast needs opening cash and explicit commitments/assumptions. Generic POS records do not prove vehicle acquisition/reconditioning/floorplan costs, ingredient waste, expiry lots, or operating profit.

Scheduled POS providers currently include R-Series, X-Series, Square, Clover, Shopify, Shopify POS, Moneris and Stripe. The default scheduling function uses 900 seconds after a completed run, 60 seconds for continuation, and bounded retry backoff. This is a scheduling policy, not a measured customer freshness SLA. Google, Meta and Slack are not in that scheduled POS list. Provider webhooks are signals requiring authenticated, idempotent processing and reconciliation; their existence does not prove every historical correction is covered.

## First-launch connectors

### Lightspeed Retail POS R-Series

**Provider.** R-Series Retail API V3 is distinct from X-Series. It uses OAuth 2.0 and account-scoped resources, with V3 cursor pagination and at most 100 objects per page. Items can be archived rather than physically deleted; default active-item lists are not a complete historical product register. The exact eligible merchant plan, country, third-party distribution approval and contractual history/retention limits for Vanteloq remain unverified. [API introduction](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/introduction/introduction/), [OAuth](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/authentication/authentication-overview/), [scopes](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/authentication/scopes/), [pagination](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/introduction/pagination/).

**Vanteloq.** `lightspeed-r.ts` pins V3 and requests `employee:register_read` and `employee:inventory_read`. The importer covers sales, lines, items, customers, suppliers, shops, payment types and supplied stock/cost fields. It tracks collection cursors separately, requires collection completion for completed backfill, and has a recent 24-hour sweep. Shop selection/mapping and subsequent review remain essential. Provider quantities, costs, tax, returns, timestamps and source currency must be retained with their original meaning; missing cost is not zero cost. No universal historical depth is established by this local backfill mechanism.

**Launch evidence still needed.** An unrelated merchant with supported R-Series access must complete consent, OAuth, shop selection, resumable history, late corrections, disconnect/reconnect and independently reconciled sales/tenders. Specifically test archived items associated with historical sales, long-delayed refunds/corrections, closed versus incomplete sales, and account/shop timezone boundaries. Current files include `lightspeed-r-callback-flow.test.mjs`, `lightspeed-r-recent-sync.test.ts` and `lightspeed-commerce.test.ts`. Do not infer webhooks or full deleted-record reconciliation from the polling path. Applicable terms and logo permission are open; the existing [Lightspeed privacy policy](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/legal/privacy-policy/) link is shared with X-Series.

### Square

**Provider.** Square OAuth grants access to the consenting seller. Orders and Refunds are different financial objects: a payment refund is not automatically a matched item return or a sale-period revenue reversal. Money uses currency-specific minor units. OAuth production requirements and seller/account capability must be checked separately from Marketplace listing. The official reference currently exposes version 2026-09-16; Vanteloq deliberately pins 2026-07-15, which needs a supported-version watch rather than an automatic upgrade. [API reference](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square), [production OAuth](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/oauth-api/movetoprod), [orders](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/orders), [returns/exchanges](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/orders-api/order-returns-exchanges), [refund events](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/refunds-api/webhooks), [money](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/build-basics/common-data-types/working-with-monetary-amounts).

**Vanteloq.** Read scopes are `MERCHANT_PROFILE_READ`, `ORDERS_READ`, `PAYMENTS_READ`, `CUSTOMERS_READ`, `ITEMS_READ`, `INVENTORY_READ`. Selected locations feed a cursor-based import; the local initial order window is two years, with a five-minute incremental overlap. That is an implementation window, not a provider-wide maximum. Current source separates sale/return financial events, validates currency/tax/tips, and blocks unsupported reconstruction. The October 1 [financial review](financial-final-review-2026-10-01.md) records isolated tests, not a live merchant acceptance test. Payment settlement, bank cash and POS revenue remain distinct.

**Launch evidence still needed.** Confirm grant, countries/account types and actual populated fields for an unrelated seller; reconcile mixed sales/returns, unlinked refunds, cancellation and late updated orders, tips/taxes, multi-location and non-two-decimal currencies. Inspect signature/replay and revoked OAuth behavior. Test anchors: `square.test.ts`, `square-financials.test.ts`, `square-sync-flow.test.mjs`. Exact stock-cost/history coverage remains data-dependent. Use [Canadian privacy terms](https://squareup.com/ca/en/legal/general/privacy) and the applicable [developer integration marketing agreement](https://squareup.com/ca/en/legal/general/global-developer-integration-marketing-agreement?country_redirection=true). An official asset hub or US trademark licence does not establish that Vanteloq has the Canadian agreement or an endorsement badge right.

### Slack

**Provider and implementation.** OAuth v2 `incoming-webhook` lets the customer choose a channel. Vanteloq requests and validates exactly that scope, stores an encrypted webhook, and offers a confirmed data-free test or secure sign-in link. It does not read conversations/files, import financial entities, or run automatic alerts. Message-history scopes, Events API quotas and Marketplace history-rate rules are therefore not capabilities of this connector. [Scope](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/scopes/incoming-webhook), [incoming webhooks](https://api.slack.com/messaging/webhooks).

**Open requirements.** Confirm workspace policy permits installation and external commercial distribution, channel choice belongs to this customer, a revoked/deleted channel fails clearly, and disconnect stops sends. `slack.test.ts`, `slack-worker-runtime.test.mjs`, `slack-sharing-flow.test.mjs` are fixture anchors. There is no sales history, currency, refund or accounting capability to advertise. Slack's current developer policy requires an accessible privacy policy and specifies deletion of associated API data within 14 business days after discontinuing/removing the app; verify how encrypted credentials, channel identifiers, receipts and audit retention meet the applicable contract rather than assuming the general retention table proves it. [Developer policy](https://docs.slack.dev/developer-policy/), [API terms](https://slack.com/terms-of-service/api), [privacy](https://slack.com/trust/privacy/privacy-policy). Logo use and app-directory approval evidence remain open.

### Manual imports and document forwarding

Manual daily-summary CSV, bank-statement/document paths and document forwarding complement unavailable APIs. They do not inherit the originating provider's OAuth grant or provide automatic source history. Preserve original file/document provenance, uploader and organization, currency, period, mapped locations, calculation version, duplicate identity, validation errors and approval decisions. Corrections require traceable replacement/versioning; a re-upload must not create duplicate revenue. A bank deposit or extracted invoice is not automatically a posted entry.

The owner-reported email enable/verified flags do not prove this turn's hosted scan, malware quarantine, inbound delivery or successful extraction. No live email was sent in this review. Existing anchors include `daily-metric-import.test.ts`, document provider/ingest tests and BookLoQ tests; the exact allowed import schemas are authoritative. Manually supplied missing costs, expiry lots or dealership data need their own validated templates before corresponding metrics can be supported. Retention, document storage and backup limitations are in [DATA_RETENTION](DATA_RETENTION.md) and the [operations review](operations-policy-review-2026-10-01.md).

### Plaid

Plaid Transactions/Link, local API header `2020-09-14`, provides authorized selected financial accounts and transaction changes. Initial history defaults to 90 days and can request up to 730, subject to institution; ongoing history accumulates, so “all history is capped at two years” would be wrong. Added, modified and removed transactions must be processed across the whole update; pending-to-posted may be removal plus addition on different pages. Institution updates are not real-time guarantees. [Transactions](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/), [API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/), [transaction lifecycle](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/transactions-data/).

Vanteloq is sandbox-gated with balances/transactions, Link account selection, cursor sync, verified webhooks and finance review. A bank transaction is cash evidence, not a categorization or sale. Preserve ISO/unofficial currency, sign conventions, date versus authorization time, transfer identity and pending status. Production product access and actual Canadian institution/account coverage, history and reauthorization still need proof. `plaid.test.ts` and relevant banking flows are fixture anchors. [Plaid legal/developer and end-user privacy policies](https://plaid.com/legal/) need to be tied to the production agreement, disconnect/Item removal, retention, deletion and permissible AI use before launch. No logo permission is established by sandbox access.

### October 1 implementation: account and field readiness
