# BookLoQ benchmark and implementation priorities

Reviewed October 4, 2026 against the supplied full BookLoQ master brief. This is a static source audit and primary-document benchmark. No application, database, provider connection, build, or hosted release was exercised. “Implemented” below means a corresponding code path exists, not that it passed end-to-end verification. This supplements the existing September 19 competitive benchmark in `docs/research/bookloq-competitive-benchmark-2026-09-19.md`.

BookLoQ has useful accounting infrastructure to extend. The next phase should complete the path from reviewed source evidence to a posted entry, statement reconciliation, period report, and controlled close. Another dashboard rebuild would leave the largest accounting gaps unresolved. No evidence supports a superiority claim over established accounting products.

## Canadian tax architecture requirements

The following are architecture conclusions from current official guidance, not a determination of any particular business's tax treatment.

1. **Keep federal GST/HST rules separate from provincial tax adapters.** GST/HST treatment depends on supply type and place of supply, not simply the owner's home province. Rates and transitional rules need effective dates and preserved transaction snapshots. CRA also requires invoice information supporting customer ITC claims. [CRA charging and collecting guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)

2. **Represent taxable, zero-rated and exempt explicitly.** Two lines with zero tax can have different recovery consequences. Revenu Québec connects supply classification to both collection and ITC/ITR eligibility. Store registration, tax component, recoverability and supporting evidence separately from the numeric rate. [Revenu Québec supply types](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/basic-rules-for-applying-the-gsthst-and-qst/types-of-supplies/)

3. **Fix precision before claiming complete Quebec support.** `domain/invoice-amounts.ts:13`, `domain/invoice.ts:108` and `db/schema.ts:2020` accept integer basis points and one rate per invoice line. Exact QST at 9.975% requires 997.5 basis points. Revenu Québec specifies the three-decimal rate and rounding guidance. Use an exact decimal/rational or finer integer scale, distinct GST/QST components and explicit rounding policy. Do not silently round to 9.98% or label a combined rate as QST. [Revenu Québec calculation rules](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/collecting-gst-and-qst/calculating-the-taxes/)

4. **PST is a separate provincial regime.** For example, B.C. PST applies to taxable goods, software and services subject to specific exemptions. Its obligations include tax owed on some business-use purchases. Do not generalize GST-style input credits or one province's exemptions to all provincial taxes. [B.C. PST guidance](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/pst)

5. **Separate recorded tax, return preparation, filing and remittance.** The current BookLoQ helper derives net tax from `gst_collected` and `gst_recoverable` ledger accounts. That is useful ledger evidence, not a jurisdiction-complete return. A tax workspace needs period reconciliation, adjustments, authority-specific return mappings, review and retained filing/payment evidence. Provider credentials or authorization cannot be inferred from an available button.

## Recommended delivery order

| Priority | Concrete deliverable | Evidence needed before calling it complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Accounting correctness** | Exact multi-component tax representation; period-scoped close; defined document recognition and payment-allocation contracts. | QST precision/rounding cases, mixed supply treatments, balance and reversal checks, cross-organization and locked-period rejection, retry/concurrency checks. |
| **2. One complete reconciliation journey** | Statement opening/closing inputs, selected cleared entries, remaining difference, saved progress, supporting statement, completion report and controlled reopen. | Missing/duplicate transactions, opening-balance mismatch, partial settlement, transfer and outstanding-item cases; no silent balancing adjustment. |
| **3. Traceable reports and handover** | Period P&L, as-of balance sheet/TB, GL drilldown, AR/AP aging linkage, source metadata and reliable exports. | Each total ties to the same posted ledger; previous-period comparison uses equal definitions; exports retain period, currency, basis and filters. |
| **4. Owner collection/payment work** | Invoice issue/approval, payment allocation, partials, credits/refunds and bill review with clear accounting consequences. | A document, an external payment and a posted journal remain distinguishable and cannot create duplicate revenue or cash. External payment initiation remains separately authorized. |
| **5. Migration and planning completeness** | CSV/Excel mapping with row errors, reviewed opening balances and control totals; then forecast coverage, tax/payroll schedules and saved scenarios. | Re-import is safe; rejected rows are retained; opening TB and open AR/AP reconcile; unavailable sources remain unavailable rather than zero. |

Start the next implementation phase with a bounded source-to-close vertical slice and retained fixtures. Runtime permission, persistence, accessibility, mobile and accounting verification remain outstanding for this audit. No code changes, provider calls, publication or deployment were performed.
